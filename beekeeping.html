Learning Through Direct Experience

Beekeeping and Conservation offers visitors a rare, up-close introduction to beekeeping — led by our resident beekeepers. Sessions cover hive inspection, colony behavior, and honey harvesting, giving participants a genuine understanding of what it takes to maintain a healthy, thriving hive. Protective gear is provided, and no prior experience is needed.
