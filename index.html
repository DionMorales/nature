Small Wings, Lasting Impact.

Zone 1

Butterfly Sanctuary

Explore a glass-domed sanctuary home to native butterflies and honeybee colonies, built to support pollinator research and public education in the highlands of Benguet.

Zone 2

Beekeeping & Conservation

Learn directly from our resident beekeepers — from hive inspection and honey harvesting to understanding colony health and pollinator ecology. Sessions run year-round for students, researchers, families, and aspiring beekeepers.

Zone 3

Research Facilities

Our labs support ongoing studies on butterfly and bee populations, habitat health, and climate impact — open to student researchers, partner institutions, and public tours.
