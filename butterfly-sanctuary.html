A Sanctuary Built Around Nature

The Butterfly Sanctuary was designed as more than an exhibit — it’s a functioning habitat. Its glass-domed structure allows natural light and airflow to sustain a self-contained ecosystem, home to native butterfly species, nectar-rich plants, and seasonal blooms. Every design choice, from the greenhouse framing to the garden layout, was made to support butterfly life cycles while giving visitors an immersive, close-up view of pollinators in their natural behavior.
